How to Calculate Sound Dispersion for Glass Atriums and Outdoor Venues

ER
Elena Rostova
Lead Sound Engineer, SonicDrop Audio

Every audio engineer dreads hearing the words: "Our reception is in a modern architectural venue featuring 30-foot floor-to-ceiling glass windows and marble floors." Glass and polished stone possess absorption coefficients near zero, creating reverberation times exceeding 3.5 seconds.

  • Deploy distributed arrays at 45-60ft intervals.
  • Outdoor SPL drops 6dB per doubling distance.
