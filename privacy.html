SonicDrop Privacy Policy

We respect your event privacy and personal records. Learn how our booking platform, audio engineering consultation tools, and event portals safeguard your data.

Privacy Officer Inquiries

SECTION 01

1. Information We Collect

When booking audio equipment, reserving resident DJs, or submitting consultation requests via our online forms, SonicDrop collects the necessary information to draft technical production riders and coordinate logistics:

  • Contact Details: Host name, organizer title, telephone number, and email address.
  • Event Logistics: Event date, venue address, indoor/outdoor classification, load-in freight elevator access, and expected guest count.
  • Musical & Acoustic Preferences: Custom song playlists, "Do Not Play" lists, first dance song selections, and speech schedules.
  • Technical Riders: Stage dimensions, venue power distribution specifications, and acoustic decibel ceilings.
SECTION 02

2. How We Use Event Data

We use your submitted event parameters strictly to provide commercial-grade sound engineering and entertainment services. We do not sell, rent, or monetize client records to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

Your venue specifications and speech schedules are shared solely with your assigned front-of-house (FOH) audio technician, stage rigger, and resident DJ talent for rehearsal and load-in execution.

SECTION 03

3. Audio & Speech Recordings

When clients book speech audio recording packages (e.g. Wedding vow captures, corporate keynote recordings), master WAV recordings are captured directly from our digital mixer console.

Client Ownership & Confidentiality

All speech and musical recordings remain the exclusive property of the client. SonicDrop retains a private encrypted backup for 30 days post-event to allow client download, after which master files are permanently scrubbed unless archival storage is requested in writing.

SECTION 04

4. Payment & Retainer Security

All payment transactions, booking retainers, and security deposits are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways. SonicDrop servers never store full credit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking credentials.

SECTION 05

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes browser localStorage strictly for functional preferences: saving your Dark/Light theme mode, RTL/LTR layout toggle, and temporary contact form field drafts. We do not employ third-party behavioral ad trackers.

SECTION 06

6. Your Rights & Data Erasure

You maintain full rights to review, update, or request the complete deletion of your event history and contact details from our customer database at any time. Simply submit a deletion request to privacy@sonicdrop.audio and our records will be purged within 48 business hours.
