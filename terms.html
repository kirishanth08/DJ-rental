ARTICLE 01

1. Booking Retainer & Payment Schedule

To lock in your requested date on our production calendar, a 30% non-refundable retainer is required at the time of contract execution. The remaining 70% balance is due 14 calendar days prior to the scheduled event date.

For bookings secured within 14 days of the performance date, 100% of the invoice balance is payable upon signing.

ARTICLE 02

2. Delivery, Setup & Soundcheck Logistics

Our audio technicians and resident DJs arrive at the venue 3 to 4 hours prior to guest arrival for comprehensive room acoustic tuning, cable tucking, and wireless microphone frequency scans.

  • The client or venue manager must provide unimpeded freight access and elevator availability during load-in.
  • Post-event breakdown (strike) begins immediately following the final music cue and typically requires 90 to 120 minutes.
ARTICLE 03

3. Hardware Care & Damage Liability

SonicDrop deploys commercial touring hardware (Pioneer CDJ-3000, L-Acoustics, Shure Axient Digital). Our crew retains supervision over the gear throughout the event.

Guest Liquid & Physical Damage

The hiring client is financially responsible for physical damage or liquid spills caused by event attendees to our DJ facade, microphones, or floor speakers during the rental duration.

ARTICLE 04

4. Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy

We understand that schedules and milestones shift. SonicDrop provides flexible postponement policies:

  • 60+ Days Prior: Full retainer may be credited toward any available date within 12 months.
  • 30-59 Days Prior: 50% of the retainer is creditable to a new date.
  • Under 30 Days: Retainer is forfeited to cover reserved artist calendar holds and technician dispatches.
ARTICLE 05

5. Outdoor Event & Rain Contingency

Outdoor audio and lighting rigs require overhead shelter (tent or permanent marquee) covering 100% of the DJ booth and electronic consoles. In the event of heavy rain or lightning, equipment will be powered down to prevent electrical hazards until safe shelter is verified.

ARTICLE 06

6. Electrical Power Standards

The client is responsible for supplying dedicated 120V / 20-amp grounded AC circuits within 50 feet of the performance area. For festival stages and large outdoor line arrays, SonicDrop can provide quiet silent inverter generators upon advance request.
